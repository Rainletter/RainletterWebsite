Your journal, your way

Every detail designed to feel like opening a notebook — quiet, personal, and entirely yours.

AI that listens

A gentle, on-device journal agent that helps you reflect — asks the right questions, notices patterns, and never judges. All processing happens on your phone.

Completely private

No servers. No accounts. No analytics. Your entries live on your device — or in your personal iCloud if you choose. We never see your data.

Mood tracking

Nine hand-drawn mood icons — from happy to anxious to chill — let you mark how you feel in a single tap. See patterns over days, weeks, and months.

Calendar & entries

Switch between a list of entries and a quiet calendar grid. Photos fill the day cells; mood icons float behind dates. Events and reminders sit alongside your writing.

Works offline

No Wi-Fi required. The AI runs entirely on-device — write on a plane, in the woods, or anywhere your thoughts find you. Your journal is always ready.

Designed with care

Every pixel considered. Spectral serif for your words, hairline icons, generous whitespace. Four themes — default, notebook, terminal, glass — each crafted to match how you feel.

Privacy is the architecture,
not a setting.

DearSelf has no server. The AI model runs on Apple's Neural Engine, right on your device. Your journal entries never travel over a network — they stay in local storage or your personal iCloud, at your choice. There is nothing to breach because there is nothing to reach.
